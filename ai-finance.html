How data validation works

Each bank transaction is compared with its ledger record in a fixed order. The first rule that fails sets the status. These are transparent demo rules, not the original project’s reconciliation logic.

RuleCheckResult
1A ledger entry exists for the bank transactionUnmatched if not
2Bank and ledger amounts are equalAmount mismatch if not
3Bank and ledger dates are within 2 daysDate mismatch if not
4All checks passMatched
